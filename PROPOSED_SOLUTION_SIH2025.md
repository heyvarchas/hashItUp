# SIH 2025 — Slide 2: Proposed Solution (Simple & Clear)

---

### **IDEA TITLE**
**AI-Powered Stress & Fatigue Monitering System for Armed Forces**

---

### **❖ Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)**

* **Detailed explanation of the proposed solution**
  * **Daily Quick Check-in:** Soldiers do a 30-second daily check-in on mood, sleep, and stress.
  * **Smart Data Fusion:** The system tracks work hours, night shifts, missed leave, and tough deployment conditions alongside their check-ins.
  * **100% Secret & Private:** Personal names/IDs are locked away separately. The AI only sees anonymous code numbers, so nobody fears judgment.
  * **Early Warning Score (0–100):** AI calculates a fatigue score and instantly alerts welfare officers if someone is burning out or asks for help.

* **How it addresses the problem**
  * **Catches Problems Early:** Finds exhaustion weeks before a soldier breaks down or gets sick.
  * **Removes Fear & Stigma:** Soldiers can be honest because their names are kept completely private.
  * **Clear Steps to Help:** Tells officers the exact reason for distress and recommends actions like mandatory rest, leave, or counseling.

* **Innovation and uniqueness of the solution**
  * **Hybrid AI + Hard Safety Rules:** Uses AI for smart scoring, but if a soldier asks for help, emergency rules kick in immediately—no AI mistakes.
  * **Plain-Language Explanations:** Explains reasons simply (e.g., *"5 night shifts in a row"*, *"No leave for 90 days"*).
  * **Built-in Action Tracker:** Ensures every alert is reviewed, treated, and recorded by an officer.

---

### 🎙️ 20-Second Presentation Script
> *"Soldiers face extreme stress and long shifts, but often hide it due to fear or stigma. Our system gives soldiers a 100% private, anonymous check-in. It combines their mood and sleep with duty and leave records to predict burnout before it happens. Officers get clear alerts and practical steps—like mandatory rest or counseling—to protect our troops early."*
