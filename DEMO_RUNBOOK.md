## 3. Step-by-Step Live Walkthrough Script

### Act I: The Scripted Personnel Check-in
1. **Navigate to Login:** Open `http://localhost:5173/login`.
2. **Sign In as Personnel:**
   - Click **`CAPF-2024-001` (Personnel Role)** quick button (or enter `CAPF-2024-001` / `password123`).
   - Click **Sign In**. You will land on the **Personnel Self-Service Dashboard** (`/personnel`).
3. **Inspect Baseline State:**
   - Notice the supportive, non-clinical framing: Workload Status is *Moderate*, prior check-in history shows steady ratings (Mood 4/5).
4. **Initiate Check-in:**
   - Click **Submit Daily Wellness Check-in** (`/personnel/checkin`).
5. **Submit the Tipping Check-in:**
   - *Option 1 (1-Click):* Click the blue **Pre-fill Demo Scenario** banner button.
   - *Option 2 (Manual):*
     - Mood Rating: **1 / 5** (Distressed)
     - Sleep Quality: **1 / 5** (Broken / Insomnia)
     - Stress Self-Rating: **9 / 10** (Severe / Overwhelming)
     - Note: *"Severe exhaustion, chronic sleep disturbance and feeling unable to cope."*
   - Click **Submit Confidential Assessment**.
   - A green confirmation box will confirm the write with assessment ID and pseudonymous ID.

---

### Act II: Welfare Officer Alert Triage
1. **Log Out & Sign In as Officer:**
   - Return to `http://localhost:5173/login`.
   - Click **`CAPF-2024-002` (Welfare Officer Role)** quick button (or enter `CAPF-2024-002` / `password456`).
   - Click **Sign In**.
2. **Review Unit Aggregate Dashboard (`/welfare`):**
   - Highlight the privacy-preserving population distribution bar chart and the **Active Open Alerts** badge.
3. **Open the Alerts Queue (`/welfare/alerts`):**
   - Click **Review Active Alerts** in the top navigation or banner.
   - You will see the new **HIGH SEVERITY ALERT** at the top of the queue:
     - Calibrated Score: **$\ge 65/100$ (High Tier)**
     - Trigger: *Sudden drop in self-reported wellness score ($\ge 2$ points)*
     - Status: `OPEN` (Amber/Rose badge)

---

### Act III: Clinical Drill-Down & Case Review
1. **Open Confidential Case File:**
   - Click **Review Case File** on the active alert card (`/welfare/case?pseudonymous_id=...`).
2. **Examine Explainability & Recommendations:**
   - Point out that **PII remains strictly pseudonymized** (Zero PII leakage).
   - Show the **Automated Clinical Recommendations** generated by the clinical rule engine:
     - *Counseling Referral*
     - *Emergency Rest / Leave*
     - *Duty Modification*
   - Show the **SHAP / Contributing Factors Breakdown** explaining why the score spiked.

---

### Act IV: Recording the Intervention
1. **Document Supportive Action:**
   - Scroll down to the **Record Supportive / Clinical Intervention** panel.
   - Select **Counseling Referral** (or *Duty Reassignment*).
   - Action / Clinical Notes: *"Met with individual confidentially; arranged 1-on-1 counseling and temporary night shift exemption."*
   - Target Alert Status: Select **Resolved** (or *Acknowledged*).
   - Click **Record Intervention & Update Case**.
2. **Confirm Status Transition:**
   - A success banner confirms the intervention was recorded.
   - The alert badge immediately updates to **RESOLVED**.
   - The intervention is permanently logged with officer timestamp in the **Case Intervention History** audit trail.
3. **Return to Unit Dashboard:**
   - Click **Back to Overview** (`/welfare`).
   - The open alerts count has dynamically updated back to baseline.

## 5. Summary of Architecture Invariants Demonstrated

1. **Strict Cryptographic Pseudonymization:** Identity data (`identity` schema) and behavioral stress metrics (`analytics` schema) remain strictly separated.
2. **Hybrid ML + Clinical Rule Safety Overrides:** The calibrated XGBoost model predicts continuous 0–100 risk, while deterministic safety rules enforce escalation on critical triggers (sudden drops, explicit help requests).
3. **Audit-Log Traceability:** Every triage transition and clinical intervention binds the officer's ID and timestamp for defense compliance.
